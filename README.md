# 🚀 StudyPal

**StudyPal** is an AI-powered study assistant that transforms your study materials into interactive learning experiences.

Upload your documents and instantly switch between multiple learning modes like Q&A, quizzes, flashcards, and summaries — all in one place.

---

## 🧠 Core Features

### 📂 Smart Document Upload

* Drag & drop support
* Supports **PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT**
* Processes large files into usable study chunks

---

### ❓ Q/A Mode

* Ask questions directly from your uploaded material
* Context-aware AI answers
* Great for quick concept clarification

---

### 🧪 Quiz Mode

* Auto-generated quizzes from your content
* Helps test understanding instead of passive reading

---

### 🧠 Flashcards Mode

* AI-generated flashcards by topic
* Reveal answers interactively
* Shuffle + navigation support

---

### 📝 Summarization

* Converts long documents into concise summaries
* Ideal for quick revision

---

### 📅 Daily Study Planner

* Organize your study workflow
* Keep track of what to focus on

---

## ⚡ How It Works

1. Upload your study material
2. StudyPal breaks it into chunks
3. Choose a mode:

   * Q/A
   * Quiz
   * Flashcards
   * Summary
4. Learn actively instead of passively
